How to approach this problem
This is a tree recursion problem. Your implementation should try different ways
of summing up
n
different perfect squares to see if the sum is
total
. For
the example
perfect_fit(10, 2)
, the call to
fit_from(10, 2, 1)
returns whether there
are 2 perfect squares that sum to 10 and are each
1*1
or greater. To figure
that out, you'll need to make two recursive calls:

, which returns whether there are 2 perfect squares that
sum to 10 and are each
2*2
or greater (nope)
fit_from(9, 1, 2)
, which returns whether there is 1 perfect square that sums
to 9 and is
2*2
or greater (yep:
3*3
)
Once your implementation makes these two recursive calls, it needs to combine
them into one result.
Hint
Use

in the sum and
one that does not. The first should subtract

from

and subtract 1
from

and
n
