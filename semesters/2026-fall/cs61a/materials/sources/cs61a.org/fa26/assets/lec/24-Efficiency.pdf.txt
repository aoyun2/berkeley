Appending, assigning, and list comprehensions are fast:

   3 5 2 7
         9 4 2 6                   2 4 6 8

Inserting (beginning/middle), slicing, and adding lists are slow:

   3 5 2 9
         7 3
           7 4
             3 2
               4 2                 3 5 2 7      +   9               3 5 2 7 9
