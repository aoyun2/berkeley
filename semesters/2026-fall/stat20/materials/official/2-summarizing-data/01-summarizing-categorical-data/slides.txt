What were the dimensions of the raw data from which this table was constructed?
2 columns (marching band or not, year in school)
60 rows (we “observed” 60 students total)
[Image: ; ]
How would you characterize the association between these two variables? (year in school, marching band status)
They are unrelated (no association).
Knowing one variable tells you nothing about the other.
No matter what year in school we focus on, 2/5 of them are in marching band.
Likewise, if we focus on only marching band students, 1/2 are seniors. If we focus on only non-marching-band students… still 1/2 are seniors.

This is a confusing stacked bar chart!
The height of each total bar appears to be the energy supply from renewables worldwide in each of these years.
The proportion on top, however, appears to be that number divided by the global total energy supply, which changes every year.
The fact that those proportions are decreasing left to right as the bar heights are increasing suggests that renewables are increasing every year (the numerator) but the total energy supply (the denominator) is increasing at a faster rate.
