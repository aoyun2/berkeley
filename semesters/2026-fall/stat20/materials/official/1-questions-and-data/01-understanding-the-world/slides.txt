Takeaways from this exercise
We can call the process of:
having a question,
finding data to investigate that question,
reaching a conclusion,
and then thinking of a next step which starts everything over again
the data science lifecycle.
This lifecycle involves constructing and critiquing claims made using data: which is the main goal of our course!

Course Structure
A fair concern
“Does flipped classroom just mean
I have to do more work?”
In a typical class…
In class
The instructor explains.
You listen.
At home
You try to use it.
You do the work.

The hardest part happens at home—
when nobody is there to help.
So we move the work

Answer the reading questions.
Come prepared to begin.

Solve problems.
Work on assignments.
Get help while you work.

DONE with your work
when you leave class!
