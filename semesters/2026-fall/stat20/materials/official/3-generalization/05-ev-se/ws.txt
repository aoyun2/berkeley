Hint: You can simulate a random variable by drawing from a box of tickets. Choose the tickets so that each
value appears in the right proportion, use rep() to build the box, and draw with replacement. For example,
a coin that lands heads with probability 0.7 can be represented by a box of 10 tickets, 7 marked "H" and 3
marked "T":
box <- c(rep("H", 7), rep("T", 3))

and sample(box, size = 5, replace = TRUE) simulates 5 tosses of this coin.
