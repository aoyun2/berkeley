Start of Block: PEOPLE’S PARK PROJECT - AIDED


Transition Thanks for your answers so far. We only have a few more important questions for
you. Before you answer, we’d like to give you some up-to-date information about the People’s
Park Project:

To address the on-going student housing crisis and continue supporting our unhoused
community, the University is planning to re-develop People’s Park to include the following:

   •   Undergraduate student housing
   •   Supportive housing for un-housed people and lower-income residents
   •   Continuation of onsite social services support
   •   Green/open space
   •   Commemoration of the legacy of People’s Park

Please make sure to read the above information carefully before proceeding as the next
questions will ask you about your thoughts concerning these details.
