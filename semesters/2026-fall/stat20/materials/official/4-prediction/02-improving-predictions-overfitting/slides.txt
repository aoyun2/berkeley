The model in C makes smaller errors than E in absolute terms.
But the model in E improves much more over the baseline!
The model in C is almost useless.
The Moral of the Story
MSE/RMSE measure how good your predictions are in absolute terms. They have units of measurement.
MSE behaves better as a mathematical object and is important theoretically.
RMSE behaves worse because of the square root, but is more intuitive to a human. (It has the same units as the \(Y\) variable)
\(R^2\) measures how good your predictions are relative to the baseline (or the variance of the \(Y\) variable). How impressive your model is. It will forgive you a little if your data has a lot of variation. It’s a proportion, so it has no units.
