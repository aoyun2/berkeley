Office Hours and Group Tutoring
Please stop by introduce yourself! Stat 20 offers two types of session to ask chat about the course in person but outside of class.
Instructor office hours
These are useful for to discuss topics that come in the reading or broader questions about statistics and data science. They are also a fine place to ask questions about the assignments you’re working on. Please attend only your instructor’s office hours.
Group tutoring (GT)
These sessions are a great place to work on your assignments with fellow students and seek the help of tutors whenever you get stuck. One thing to know: tutors will expect that you have done the reading and generally been attending class; this is not meant to replace class time.
Weekly GT times
5-7pm, Monday through Thursday
You’re welcome to attend whichever GT sessions work best for your schedule.
